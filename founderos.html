Business Performance & Management Decisions

FounderOS

Clarity for weekly management decisions

Brings operating information together for a weekly business review, helping management see what changed, what needs attention, and who should follow up.

Working portfolio prototype · Fictional demonstration data

The business problem

As a startup grows, sales, support, onboarding, and revenue information sits with different teams. Each team may know its own position, but management still needs a coherent view. Without it, important changes can be missed and issues discussed without clear ownership.

My approach

The fictional RelayOps scenario is framed around a founder preparing for a weekly management meeting: how is the business performing, what needs attention, and what decisions or follow-ups are needed? Bringing numbers together is only the first step. The workspace connects reporting with action tracking so an issue can lead to an owner, a deadline, and a record of progress.

What I built

  • Bring departmental records into a consistent management view, with checks and a preview before imports are confirmed.
  • Compare revenue, sales, support, and onboarding performance across reporting periods.
  • Prepare and edit a weekly business brief for review, with PDF, HTML, and Markdown exports.
  • Turn issues into follow-ups with owners, deadlines, status, and history.

The core walkthrough works without an AI model. Optional AI commentary is checked against recorded evidence, with a labelled deterministic fallback.
