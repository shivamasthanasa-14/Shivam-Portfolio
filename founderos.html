Business Reporting & Automation

FounderOS

From records to decisions

A reporting workspace that validates raw records, calculates performance indicators, surfaces operational issues, and prepares an editable weekly brief.

Working portfolio prototype · Fictional demonstration data

The business problem

Departmental files contain useful information, but bringing them together into a coherent weekly management update takes repeated preparation.

Reporting context

The fictional RelayOps startup has revenue, sales, support, and onboarding records. The management questions are simple: what changed, what needs attention, and who should follow up?

What I built

  • Validate Excel records and preview imports before saving.
  • Compare business indicators across defined reporting periods.
  • Prepare an editable six-section weekly brief and export PDF, HTML, or Markdown.
  • Track follow-ups with owners, deadlines, status, and history.

The core reporting walkthrough works without an AI model. Optional AI commentary is checked against recorded evidence, with a labelled deterministic fallback.
