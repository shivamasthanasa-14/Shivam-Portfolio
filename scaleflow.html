Diagnostic Operations & Process Improvement

ScaleFlow

Where operations slow down, and why

Shows where blood-test cases are waiting in a simulated diagnostic operation and helps managers investigate recorded delays before preparing follow-ups.

Working portfolio prototype · Fictional demonstration data

The business problem

A diagnostic operation can appear busy while patients wait longer for reports. In the fictional VitaFlow Health scenario, cases move through several teams and handoffs. Overall turnaround time alone can conceal queues building at a particular stage, making it hard to distinguish an isolated delay from a recurring operating constraint.

My approach

The analysis follows the complete blood-testing journey, looking at where cases wait, where work accumulates, and how demand or disruptions change the picture. Stage-level waiting is considered alongside completed turnaround because an overall average can hide a developing bottleneck. Recorded histories and exception notes help investigate possible causes; they do not establish a cause where evidence is missing.

The operating process

This is the blood-testing service pathway being monitored, rather than an AI investigation workflow.

  1. 01Test requested→
  2. 02Sample collection→
  3. 03Sample received→
  4. 04Laboratory processing→
  5. 05Medical review→
  6. 06Report delivered

Cases may wait because of capacity constraints, incomplete handoffs, logistics disruptions, or missing operational updates. These are possible scenarios illustrated with fictional records, not claims about real healthcare companies.

What I built

  • Track cases across the diagnostic pathway and compare waiting times at each stage.
  • Review overdue work and developing bottlenecks across normal, growing-demand, and disruption scenarios.
  • Inspect individual case histories and exception notes when investigating a detected delay.
  • Prepare editable operational follow-ups with an owner and deadline; review Excel imports before analysis.
