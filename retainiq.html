AI Workflows & Customer Support

RetainIQ

Preparation with human review

A support workflow that groups recurring problems, prepares responses grounded in documentation, and keeps review and follow-up visible.

Working portfolio prototype · Fictional demonstration data

The business problem

A fictional company receives recurring customer-support requests. Staff repeatedly read similar messages, check documentation, and draft replies, while unusual or unsupported cases need individual investigation.

Support context

Routine issues can share relevant guidance, but each customer’s circumstances still need review. RetainIQ brings repeated preparation together so the team can concentrate on decisions and exceptions, without claiming measured productivity gains.

What I built

  • Inspect workload across three fictional support scenarios and reporting windows.
  • Group recurring issues and prepare documentation-backed response drafts.
  • Review sources and edit, reject, escalate, or approve each ticket individually.
  • Assign follow-ups and record resolution notes in a separate visitor workspace.
