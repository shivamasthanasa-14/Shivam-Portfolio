Preparation with human review

A support workflow that groups recurring problems, prepares responses grounded in documentation, and keeps review and follow-up visible.

Working portfolio prototype · Fictional demonstration data

01 / The Business Problem

Recurring support requests create repeated reading, document searches, and first-draft work, while exceptions need individual attention.

My Approach

I treated support preparation and employee decisions as separate responsibilities. The workflow brings similar problems together, keeps source documents attached to each draft, and routes unsupported or complex requests to individual review.

What I Built

  • Inspect workload across three fictional support scenarios and historical reporting windows.
  • Group requests by specific issue and handling needs, with documentation-backed response drafts.
  • Inspect sources, edit, reject, escalate, or approve each ticket individually.
  • Assign follow-ups and record resolution notes in isolated SQLite workspaces.

Technical Approach

Python and Streamlit coordinate the workflow, with SQLite for records and lifecycle history. TF-IDF retrieval finds relevant knowledge documents. Optional local Ollama can process new inputs when running locally. The hosted prepared demo replays genuine recorded model outputs for exact matching inputs and needs no model at review time.

Scope & limitations

All requests and knowledge documents are fictional. Prepared results are recorded outputs, not fresh inference. Approval does not send a customer message or resolve a ticket. New or changed inputs need live AI or manual review; no measured time or financial savings are claimed.
